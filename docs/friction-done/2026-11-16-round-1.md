## Common causes

Ranked by how many reports hit them and what they cost:

1. **A gate that stops at the first timeout on a shared machine** (4
   reports, ~30 min each): premerge chained its steps with `&&`, tests of
   45–60 s ran under a 60 s timeout while other agents' suites ran, and
   nothing told a timeout from a failure. Timings taken minutes apart under
   that load weren't comparable (3 reports).
2. **The worktree sandbox's rules were undocumented** (all 8 reports, 5–10
   refused commands each): `source`, heredocs, `cd … && git` chains, loops
   around git; and there was no in-tree place for scratch scripts that could
   import the packages without breaking lint and premerge.
3. **Hold-outs separated by a list, not a place** (3 reports): any glob over
   fixtures/pending touched them; every scan needed its own skip list.
4. **Tools made for human verdicts, data thrown away** (3 reports): no
   per-hit export of the log check; the importer kept only what damage
   needs; the raw battle detail was discarded after import; no tool to read
   BuffData trees.
5. **The engine records only what damage reads** (2 reports): who applied
   an infliction, which cast a hit belonged to, which press gave a buff, the
   inputs to recompute a hit: each analysis reconstructed them with
   heuristics.
6. **Knowledge scattered or implicit** (3 reports): enum readings spread
   over kit analyses; what the data can't answer not written down; per-status
   own-hit rules as ad hoc branches (which hid the Breach bug).
7. **Generated files merged by hand, duplicated helpers** (4 reports): built
   journal files conflicting, snapshots failing mid-merge, four stat-label
   maps, copied test helpers, deep imports of timeline internals.
8. **Rule tooling's noise and speed** (3 reports): phase 2's.
