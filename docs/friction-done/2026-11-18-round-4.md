## Common causes, ranked by what they cost accuracy work

1. **No query layer over the log itself** (eight of twelve reports; the re-triages spent about half their sessions on
   it, 10–30 throwaway scripts each). The views of round 3 answer "how does the engine see it"; triage and test
   deciding ask "what does the log say": the casts of X (combos have no cast event, ultimates' events repeat, Si's are
   hitless), a buff's runs, X near Y, who is controlled, is a key a timer. Each script re-derived loading, grouping and
   the log's habits, with silent miscounts that reached briefs as facts (three of five handed claims were wrong).
2. **Names and mappings were opaque** (six reports): operator ids (Rossi is chr_0028_wulfa), listed buff ids against
   kit buffs (common affixes, `talent1` being the second talent, weapon team buffs listed on the wielder only),
   compiled frames only through a scratch script.
3. **The lock starved short jobs and broke output** (five reports): a one-minute measure waited an hour behind rule
   searches, a `timeout` killed one in the queue without an end marker, heartbeats on stdout broke `--json`, nested
   `EFSIM-DONE` lines ended a wait early.
4. **Timing changes broke hand-pinned things far away** (three reports, two hours each): loop tests, the owner's rules
   check, kit tests pinning expiries, example timelines; 32-seed team runs for numbers that could not move.
5. **The replay's silent overrides** (three reports): a cast made against the kit, a cast inside another's uncuttable
   frames, a buff's expiry moved by stops with no trace; each looked like a kit bug for a round or two.
6. **Hold-out names one diff away** (one report, but a rule's breach): the pending verdict file held the hold-outs.
