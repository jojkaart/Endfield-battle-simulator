# Perlica's combo hits and her 9090 total (2026-11-22)

Task: diagnose Perlica's combo hits ×1.15–1.40 above the engine, her −9.6%
in 9090's totals and fc63's ×0.934 ATK, and fix what the game data
explains (branch of this worktree, base 1ab4647).

## 1. "match" verdicts that are 50% off hide a missing buff

- **What happened:** `zmdlogs groups --operator perlica` listed only 13 own
  combo mismatches; most of her combo hits were "match" at ×1.500 of the
  `predicted` value (4360, 9090, 9847), matched by the log-listing
  fallback. The cause (Pulser Labs' +0.5 not on the applying hit) only
  showed when I tabulated `logged / predicted` from `zmdlogs hits --json`
  in a scratch script. It is also what made 9090's totals −9.4% "although
  every hit matches": the totals use the primary reading, the verdict the
  fallback.
- **Cause:** `groups` and the per-operator summaries count verdicts, and a
  fallback match counts as a match; nothing groups hits by their primary
  ratio.
- **Suggestion:** in `zmdlogs groups`, add a section "matched only by the
  fallback, by skill event: median logged ÷ primary" (or a `--fallback`
  flag that treats fallback matches as mismatches), and in the check's
  per-operator line the totals' share of damage from fallback-matched hits.
- **Why it helps:** a constant ratio on fallback matches is a missing
  modifier in the replay; seeing it takes one command instead of a script.

## 2. No view of a skill's hits across logs with their zones

- **What happened:** to compare one skill's hits over ten logs I wrote
  scratch/pcombo*.py over `zmdlogs hits <log> --operator perlica --json`
  (one CLI call per log; `--all` took over two minutes and I stopped it),
  printing ratio, crit flag, scale, damageBonus, damageTaken, the engine's
  modifiers and the listed buffs. This table found both causes.
- **Cause:** `zmdlogs hits` prints one line per hit without zones; the JSON
  has them but needs a script.
- **Suggestion:** `efsim zmdlogs hits --operator <op> --event <re> --zones`
  over the development logs (from the hits cache): one line per hit with
  ratio to the primary reading, crit, and each zone that isn't 1.
- **Why it helps:** "the missing factor is additive in DMG" fell out of
  reading dB against the ratio; a built-in table makes that a minute.

## 3. "Listed" isn't "present" on the applying hit

- **What happened:** the differences view said P3, Pulser Labs and Wild
  Wanderer were "engine only" (or absent from both) on her combo hits,
  which suggested the engine had too much, the opposite of the truth: the
  log lists a buff only from the hit after the one that creates it.
- **Cause:** undocumented until now (added to docs/design/data.md,
  "Common misreadings").
- **Suggestion:** in `zmdlogs differences`, mark a difference on a hit that
  itself created the buff (its source's own trigger at that frame) as
  "created by this hit (not listable)" rather than "engine only".
- **Why it helps:** removes a misleading lead from every kit whose
  beforeDamage triggers fire on the applying hit.

## 4. A killed premerge ends with "EFSIM-DONE premerge pass"

- **What happened:** my first premerge ran past the Bash tool's 40-minute
  background limit (its test step waited for the shared lock, then its
  logs step queued behind ~70 min of others' jobs) and was terminated. Its
  test step had FAILED (two Perlica kit tests I had not updated), yet the
  log's last line read `EFSIM-DONE premerge pass`, and the Monitor waiting
  on it reported a pass.
- **Cause:** scripts/premerge.sh's `finish` trap on EXIT derives the
  verdict from `$?` alone; after the kill ("Terminated" in the log, while
  `node scripts/premerge.ts` was waiting for the lock) it was 0, and the
  test step's failure, recorded only inside premerge.ts, was lost with it.
  (Not traced further.)
- **Suggestion:** have premerge.ts write each step's result to
  scratch/runs/premerge.json as it finishes, add `trap 'killed=1' TERM INT`
  to the shell script, and let `finish` print `killed` or `fail` from
  those, `pass` only when every step reported a pass (CLAUDE.md already
  names `EFSIM-DONE <job> pass|fail|killed`).
- **Why it helps:** the DONE line is what agents wait on; a false pass
  sends a branch with failing tests to the lead.

## 5. The lock queue makes team-ab wait behind rule jobs

- **What happened:** `node scripts/team-ab.ts --base 1ab4647` waited over
  50 minutes behind other agents' `efsim rules` jobs before even the
  identity check (which runs one timeline per team) could start.
- **Cause:** team-ab takes the long-job lock for its identity check too.
- **Suggestion:** run the identity check under the short lane and take the
  long lock only for the 32-seed runs.
- **Why it helps:** most kit changes learn in a minute whether the team's
  fight moved at all.

## What worked well

`npm run measure` (one to two minutes, per-log directions, mixed logs by
per-hit error) made each hypothesis cheap to try; `zmdlogs fit-build`
settled fc63 in one run; `zmdlogs lanes` and `replay --kit-buff` showed the
buff windows directly.
