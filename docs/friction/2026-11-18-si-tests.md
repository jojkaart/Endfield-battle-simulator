# Deciding Si's in-game tests from the logs (2026-11-18)

Task: decide from the logs in-game tests 14, 41, 92 (Si), 16 (does a skill cancel a basic attack) and 15
(swapping during Gilberta's battle skill), starting from the re-triage files' claims, and fix the engine where
the logs showed it wrong. Branch: the worktree off 8c739ba (no engine change was needed).

## 1. The re-triage claims were wrong in ways a short check would have caught

- **What happened:** three of the five claims I was handed fell apart once checked.
  - Test 92: docs/in-game-tests-retriage-76-109.md said Long Heritage's team buff starts after battle skills and
    is listed on Si's hits only, which went against the engine. It had read `buff_wpn_claym_0019_teamspell` (0.168,
    the wielder's own Cryo DMG). The team buff is `buff_wpn_claym_0019_teamatk` (0.112), listed 836 and 956 times
    in the same two fixtures.
  - Test 16: the "Final Strike lands after her battle-skill cast" cases are all hitless battle-button events.
  - Test 15: "another operator's fresh chain starts inside the lock" are teammates' own attacks; Gilberta is never
    the controlled operator in any log.
  The brief repeated these claims as facts, so each one cost an hour or more to re-derive and reverse, and the lead
  planned fixes on them ("against the engine's reading. Decide and fix").
- **Cause:** the re-triage was a first look with no check against the kit. Nothing tied a listed buff key back to
  the kit buff it stands for, or a cast event to the skill's own hit. The files carry a "claims to check" caveat,
  but the brief doesn't.
- **Suggestion:**
  1. In the re-triage files, mark each provisional answer with the check it rests on: "listing only", "damage
     checked", or "engine compared".
  2. Add a "common misreadings" box to docs/design/data.md "What the log records":
     - a weapon's `_teamspell`/`_teamatk` names don't say self or team: look up the kit buff (`gameBuff`);
     - Si's `chr_0036_jsspsi_normal_skill` cast events are mostly hitless, so a real cast is one with its hit
       400–650 ms later;
     - a teammate's `attack1` isn't a swap: controlled-ness comes from basic hits that deal Stagger
       (`controlledFromLog`).
  3. Make `efsim zmdlogs raw`/`replay` print, per listed buff key, the kit buff and target it maps to.
- **Why it helps:** later passes stop reading a buff by its name or a cast event as a press, and briefs pass on the
  confidence level, not just the claim. It costs a few lines of docs and one lookup the replay already does
  (`gameBuff`).

## 2. No tool answers "who is controlled when"

- **What happened:** for test 15 I had to work out, from buffreplay.ts's comment on `controlledFromLog`, that
  basic-attack hits with `poise.dealt > 0` are controlled-only. Then I wrote scratch/t15.ts to list the controlled
  operator around each Gilberta cast. Her casts aren't logged either, so I grouped her `normal_skill` hits into
  casts by hand.
- **Cause:** `controlledFromLog` is internal to the replay. No `zmdlogs` view shows control spans or casts inferred
  from hits for operators whose casts aren't logged.
- **Suggestion:** a `efsim zmdlogs control <fixture>` view (or a column in `zmdlogs casts`) built on the exported
  `controlledFromLog`. It would list control spans (operator, from, to, evidence hit) and the placed start of every
  cast, logged or inferred.
- **Why it helps:** every swap, cancel or "while controlled" test (15, 16, 28, 54, 97, Last Rite's beside cast) needs
  this first. It's a few dozen lines over code that exists.

## 3. `zmdlogs hits --json` waited 20 minutes behind the lock, and its heartbeat went into the JSON output

- **What happened:** `node scripts/with-lock.ts node packages/cli/src/main.ts zmdlogs hits --operator
  chr_0036_jsspsi --json --workers 4 > scratch/si-hits.jsonl` queued behind six `efsim rules` jobs (about 20 min of
  waiting, then 22 min to run). with-lock's `waiting N min` heartbeat went to stdout, mixed into the JSONL, so I had
  to filter lines on `^{`. I needed only Si's combo final hits in about 20 logs.
- **Cause:**
  - with-lock prints its heartbeat on stdout, not stderr.
  - `zmdlogs hits` has no time or event filter: it checks whole logs.
  - The lock queues read-only analyses behind multi-minute rule searches.
- **Suggestion:**
  1. Send with-lock's heartbeat to stderr.
  2. Add `--event <regex>` and `--fixtures-from <file>` to `zmdlogs hits`, so a narrow question runs on fewer logs.
  3. Cache `zmdlogs hits --json` per fixture and code hash under scratch/, as `measure` does for verdicts.
- **Why it helps:** the stderr change is one line, and the output can be piped straight into a script. The cache
  would turn repeated per-hit questions (the usual way tests get decided from the logs) from tens of minutes into
  seconds.

## 4. Compiled kit frames are hard to get at

- **What happened:** to read Si's attack-5 hit frame and battle-skill hit frame (needed to interpret the
  log's gaps for test 16), I tried `data.kits` (raw JSON, no frames) and then `createContext(...).kits` through a
  scenario file. It took three attempts.
- **Cause:** frames come from SkillData at compile time. No CLI prints a compiled kit, and `efsim kit` doesn't
  exist.
- **Suggestion:** an `efsim kit <operator> [--skill <re>]` command: compiled skills with each hit's frames, poise,
  `poiseControlledOnly`, `beside`, `available`, windows and duration.
- **Why it helps:** every timing question against the log starts from these numbers; it saves a scratch script
  per agent.

## What worked well

- `checkReactions` answered test 14 in one short script.
- The fixtures' `boundaryEffects` with `source` and `since` made test 92's lanes obvious once I looked at the right
  key.
- `zmdlogs hits --json` gives per-hit `landing.modifiers[].kitBuff`, which made "does the engine have the bonus on
  this hit" a one-line filter (tests 41 and 92).
- The rule that a cause must cite its check, and the snapshots:update "No log's verdicts moved" line, made it quick
  to confirm the doc-only change was safe.
