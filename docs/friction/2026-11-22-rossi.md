# Rossi's bleed and her other mismatches (2026-11-22)

Task: diagnose and fix Rossi's (chr_0028_wulfa) mismatches, her bleed's tick clock first (pin to the log or fix the
kit), then Wolven Blood, Seething Blood and her ultimate; base 1ab4647, branch of this worktree.

## 1. `zmdlogs hits --json` fields that look like the verdict but aren't

- **What happened:** to see why bleed ticks missed I tabulated `predicted` and `error` from `zmdlogs hits --json`.
  Ticks the check counted as plain matches showed `error` −31.7% (d572 22.8 s), and `logError` was 15–54% on every
  tick; I spent a while on a wrong reading (that the engine was far off) before finding that `predicted` is the
  landing's value at the replay's crit (the replay lands null-crit hits as non-crits), the verdict is computed with the
  logged crit, and `logReading` never applies a tick snapshot except a Combustion's.
- **Cause:** the export's per-hit fields mix three readings (the replay's landing, the check's primary reading, the
  log's listing) under names that don't say which; `verdict` is "match" for every class, the class is in `class`.
- **Suggestion:** in packages/logs/src/hitexport.ts name them for what they are (`landingValue` with `landingCrit`,
  `primary` for the verdict's own reading) and add `primaryError`; document in the field comments that `logReading`
  reads a ticking buff's hit at the tick (no snapshot but Combustion's).
- **Why it helps:** the JSON is the main diagnosis input (CLAUDE.md, the scratch template); a field that reads as the
  verdict's number but isn't sends a diagnosis the wrong way.

## 2. No view of a ticking buff's ticks: logged against replayed

- **What happened:** the task's first question (does the bleed's clock drift against the log?) needed each logged tick
  against the replay's nearest tick. `zmdlogs cadence` groups hits by a 1 s gap, which merges a 1 s tick train into a
  few groups; `zmdlogs replay` shows offsets per landing but only where they match within 0.5 s. I wrote two scratch
  scripts (world-time drift per lane from `buffLanes` + `castTimeStops`, and log − replay offsets from
  `logBuffReplay(...).replay.hits`).
- **Cause:** cadence was built for timers of seconds (Liino's 10 s volleys); a per-tick comparison for any `every`
  buff isn't a command.
- **Suggestion:** `efsim zmdlogs ticks <fixture> --buff owner:id`: each logged tick of the buff's tick trigger with the
  nearest replayed tick, the offset, the steps in it over 80 ms with the placed stops between (the scratch script
  scratch/tick-offsets.ts in this worktree is the core), and the lane's world-time drift.
- **Why it helps:** every periodic kit (bleeds, Combustion, Liino's beats, future DoTs) gets the same first check in
  one command, and the "pin or fix the kit" decision has its evidence printed.

## 3. Buff windows in world time: no command

- **What happened:** for Wolven Blood's max-stack bonus and the combo's Crit DMG I needed, per run, the log's length
  (since → last listed / next unlisted) and the replay's, both with time stops taken out. That took two more scratch
  scripts (scratch/final-win.ts, scratch/combo-crit2.ts), the second inferring the crit zone each crit hit implies
  because crit buffs aren't listed.
- **Cause:** `zmdlogs lanes` gives real times only; `replay --kit-buff` gives the replay's windows in real time; neither
  takes the stops out, and nothing pairs a lane with the replay's window.
- **Suggestion:** a `--world` flag on `zmdlogs lanes` (world-time length of each run, castTimeStops) and a
  `--kit-buff owner:id` there that pairs each run with the replay's window of that kit buff (start offset, world
  length of both).
- **Why it helps:** "the game's buff lasts longer than the replay's" is a recurring lead (Twilight, Si's Amp, Rossi's
  two); the paired world-time lengths are the first number every such diagnosis needs.

## 4. Null crits are common, and nothing says so

- **What happened:** Seething Blood extras during every ultimate of Rossi had no landing; the cause was that the log
  gives her ultimate stabs no crit at all (every development log), which I found only by dumping `landing.loggedCrit`.
- **Cause:** `crit: null` is documented as "the log doesn't say" but its frequency per event isn't visible anywhere,
  and the replay's no-landing reason ("a landing whose order the kit doesn't give") doesn't mention that the trigger
  needed a crit.
- **Suggestion:** (done here: a "Common misreadings" line in docs/design/data.md, and the replay infers such crits
  where a crit trigger's hits are logged, buffreplay.ts shownCrits); still to do: make the replay's no-landing reason for a
  trigger hit say when the trigger's crit condition was unmet only because its source hit's crit is unlogged.
- **Why it helps:** 116 of Rossi's mismatches came from this; other crit-triggered kits (weapons with crit layers)
  have the same blind spot.

## 5. Waiting on the shared lock

- **What happened:** one `npm run measure` waited 10 min and ran 11 min under a full queue (another agent's batch of
  `efsim rules` jobs), past the Bash tool's 10-minute foreground limit, so it went to the background mid-run.
- **Cause:** measure is a "short" job but still queues behind long ones when several short jobs are ahead.
- **Suggestion:** say in CLAUDE.md to start `npm run measure` in the background with a Monitor on its EFSIM-DONE line
  from the start (as for snapshots), not in the foreground.
- **Why it helps:** no lost foreground calls, no duplicated runs.

## What worked well

- `zmdlogs groups --operator` gave the split (402 of 594 in the two Hard 27 logs) in one minute, and `npm run measure`
  turned each idea into the scoreboard's change in a minute when the lock was free.
- `zmdlogs lanes` (with `since`) showed at once that the bleed was applied only three times in 489c, which ruled out
  refreshes as the drift's cause.
- The fixture's per-tick listing (`effects` with values) made the snapshot question decidable from one log.
