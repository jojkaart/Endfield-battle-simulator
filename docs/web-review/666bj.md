## Differences

### W1. D28 (and D7 placement): enemy damage-reduction zone is not modelled
- **Engine:** D28: damage-reduction phases and enemy shields are not modelled [ASSUMED]. D7 has no damage-reduction zone.
- **Source:** Damage reduction (伤害减免区) is a multiplicative zone of its own, Π(1 − reduction). Named enemy cases: 破潮之像 in ranged form takes +30% Heat and −50% from other types; 碾骨先锋 takes −90% while shielded, and 棱镜天使-linked enemies take −80%. The last two sit in a "special multiplier" zone that the source says multiplies with the rest. Sections 1.4 and 1.15 of the Chinese text; English 1.4 and 1.15 (PDF).
- **Basis:** unstated (the effects are described; no test numbers given).
- **Impact if the source is right:** large on those fights: ×0.1 on shielded 碾骨先锋, ×0.2 on prism-linked enemies, ×1.3 / ×0.5 on 破潮之像 by type.
- **How we could test it:** one hit of the same operator on a shielded 碾骨先锋 and on an unshielded one (same panel); ZMDLogs hit totals for those enemies, checked against the zone product.

### W2. P6 / S3: Lift and Knock Down deal Stagger in the source
- **Engine:** P6: Lift and Knock Down deal no Stagger of their own [SETTLED], one exception in logs [OPEN]. S3: only kit hits with `poise` deal Stagger.
- **Source:** Chinese 2.2: 击飞 and 倒地 each "累积 10 点失衡值" (accumulate 10 Stagger), and each adds one 破防 stack. Chinese 2.4: the attacker's Arts Intensity raises the Stagger from 击飞/倒地 by 0.5% per point. English 1.11 says nothing about it.
- **Basis:** unstated (flat number, no test data).
- **Impact if the source is right:** every Lift or Knock Down adds 10 Stagger (about 3% of a 320-point bar, about 17% of a 60-point one), plus AI-scaled. This changes when Staggers happen in Lift-heavy rotations.
- **How we could test it:** in game, with the Stagger bar visible, use a Lift that has no poise (or read the ZMDLogs stagger field for Lift entries) and compare the bar before and after.

### W3. S10 (stagger refill): quick re-stagger reduces accumulated Stagger
- **Engine:** S10: the bar never recovers on its own; it refills to full when a Stagger ends. No reduction rule for quick re-stagger.
- **Source:** Chinese 3.5: if an enemy re-enters Stagger within a short window of its last Stagger ending (普通 3 s, 进阶 6 s, 精英/头目 10 s, 领袖 15 s), then for a window after that Stagger ends (1 s, 3 s, 5 s, 10 s) its accumulated Stagger is reduced by 50%. No numbers from a test given.
- **Basis:** unstated.
- **Impact if the source is right:** boss fights with fast re-stagger windows need up to half the Stagger in the first seconds after recovery. The engine would over-count those re-staggers.
- **How we could test it:** in game, time a boss's Stagger ends and the next Stagger with a fixed poise rotation, and compare the number of hits needed.

### W4. A36: Cryo freeze (Solidification) duration
- **Engine:** A36: Solidification lasts 5.75 / 6.75 / 7.75 / 8.75 s by level [OPEN].
- **Source:** Chinese 2.1: 冻结 (Cryo) lasts 6 / 7 / 8 / 9 s by level. The 2026/2/28 changelog entry corrects its initial damage, not its duration.
- **Basis:** unstated.
- **Impact if the source is right:** every Solidification window is 0.25 s longer per level (4% at level 1, 3% at level 4), which changes the windows for Shatter.
- **How we could test it:** check the Solidification status duration in the game data (grep the status tables for the level durations); in game, time a freeze from Solidification to its end.

### W5. R8 / A-group: Corrosion's shred scope (the two source copies disagree)
- **Engine:** R8: Corrosion's shred enters the ignore-resistance zone for every element, Physical and Ether included [SETTLED for 94–98].
- **Source:** Chinese 2.1: 腐蚀 reduces "全属性抗性" (all attributes), which matches the engine. English 1.12: "Arts resistance can be reduced by ... Corrosion", which means four arts elements only, with Ether and Physical excluded. English 1.12 says this logic was seen in some testing: a D-tier enemy with Corrosion acts as if it had negative points.
- **Basis:** Chinese text unstated. English text: "during some testing" (no numbers).
- **Impact if the English copy is right:** physical and ether hits on corroded enemies lose the 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 point shred, so the engine overstates them by 12–24% at maximum shred (the resistance factor is 1 + shred/100 in the ignore zone).
- **How we could test it:** in game, a physical hit (basic attack) on a D-tier enemy before and after Corrosion, and the same for an ether hit; ZMDLogs physical hits on corroded targets.

### W6. A21 / C4: Combustion ticks can crit in the source's general formula
- **Engine:** A21 and C4: Combustion ticks can't crit [SETTLED, from logs].
- **Source:** Chinese 2.0: the general formula for all status damage (异常伤害) includes the 暴击区 (crit zone). Nothing exempts Combustion ticks. Chinese 2.1 does not mention crit for 燃烧.
- **Basis:** formula only (unstated). The engine's rule rests on logs, which should win.
- **Impact if the source is right:** a crit-able tick would be ×1.025 expected at default crit (5% × 50%), about +2.5% of the Combustion total.
- **How we could test it:** ZMDLogs Combustion tick entries: do any carry a crit flag? If none do across many logs, the engine rule stands.

### W7. K1: SP regeneration pause after a battle skill
- **Engine:** K1: regeneration paused 1.5 s (45 frames) after any SP spend [ASSUMED; regen rate and pause [OPEN]].
- **Source:** Chinese 4.5: a battle skill cast pauses SP regeneration about 0.5 s; a perfect dodge gives about 7.5 SP and pauses regeneration about 0.5 s. The text says "测试结果显示" (test results show), so this claim has a test basis. Regeneration itself is 8 SP/s, which matches K1.
- **Basis:** in-game test (stated; values approximate).
- **Impact if the source is right:** the engine's 1 s extra pause loses about 8 SP per battle skill cast relative to the source, which adds up over a fight with frequent casts.
- **How we could test it:** in game, read the SP gauge at the cast and at 0.5 s, 1 s and 2 s afterwards; compare with the engine's trace of the same cast.

### W8. K2: the caster's own ultimate animation pauses SP regeneration
- **Engine:** K2: SP regeneration stops during others' time stops and slow-downs. The caster's own ultimate stop is not listed (T9 covers the caster's own stop for holds).
- **Source:** Chinese 4.5: "终结技动画期间也会暂停技力恢复": SP regeneration also pauses during the ultimate animation (no test numbers).
- **Basis:** unstated.
- **Impact if the source is right:** at 8 SP/s, a 1–2 s ultimate animation costs 8–16 SP per ultimate for the caster's team.
- **How we could test it:** in game, read the SP gauge across the caster's own ultimate animation, and compare with a non-ultimate stop of the same length.

### W9. S14 / N5: finisher multiplier by rank (source text disagrees with the data; engine matches the data)
- **Engine:** S14 (comment in the rule): Common 1, Advanced 1.25, Elite/Alpha 1.5, Boss 1.75 typical.
- **Source:** Chinese 3.3: 普通 1, 进阶 1.25, 精英 and 头目 1.5, 领袖 1.75. The source's own table in 3.5 gives 1.75 to 罗丹, 三位一体, 白垩界卫, 埃尔埃格尼斯 and 白垩核心, which are boss-tier, so its text and table disagree.
- **Basis:** unstated.
- **Data check (this run):** `EnemyAttributeTemplateTable.json` by `displayType` (from `EnemyTemplateDisplayInfoTable.json`): type 0 (Common) attr 27 = 1.0 (41 of 41); type 1 (Elite) = 1.5 (18 of 20); type 2 (Boss) = 1.75 (10 of 11); type 3 (Advanced) = 1.25 (14 of 14); type 4 (Alpha) = 1.5 (6 of 6). So the data agrees with the engine, and the source's text is the outlier for bosses. The engine's rank-to-name mapping (Alpha vs 领袖) is not settled by this check.
- **Impact:** none for the engine, which reads the value from the data. The engine's comment should keep "Boss 1.75" and the Alpha label should be checked.
- **How we could test it:** not needed for the engine; for the doc, check which rank the 领袖 enemies have in the data.

## Engine silent (mechanics the source states that no rule covers)

These are mechanics for the outgoing damage this engine computes, unless marked otherwise.

- **Stagger from interrupting a charge** (Chinese 3.1 item 3): interrupting an enemy's charged skill adds 15–100 Stagger depending on the skill. N8 treats charges as scenario inputs and gives no stagger from interrupts. Source has no test numbers.
- **Stagger Node stumble** (Chinese 3.4): reaching a node stumbles the enemy for 2.5 s and interrupts its action. S11 says nodes have no damage effect. Enemy actions are not simulated (N8), so no damage impact; source gives no damage effect.
- **Finisher restrictions** (Chinese 3.3): the source says Laevatain and Yvonne cannot finish a Staggered enemy while in their ultimate state, and that the finisher is the controlled operator's first basic attack. S13 has neither condition. K25 marks ultimates as [OPEN]. Check kit data.
- **Enemy DEF change below half HP** (Chinese 1.10): 碾骨行刑人 raises its DEF to 300 below 50% HP, which the source puts at about half the incoming damage. R1 holds DEF at 100 for every enemy and N6 treats HP only as a scenario input, so the engine cannot model this. Impact: ×0.5 extra damage on that enemy below half HP.
- **Composite elements and resistances** (Chinese 1.3, 1.12; English 1.12): composite damage bonus types (e.g. "Cryo and Electric") and composite resistance (法术抗性 across four arts elements, and 复合抗性). R4 uses per-element points only. Check whether any kit or gear uses a composite filter.
- **Freeze and control effects** (Chinese 2.1, 2.2): 冻结 immobilises weak enemies, 击飞 makes them airborne and 倒地 knocks them down. A37 and P13 give no control effect. No damage impact in the engine, which has no enemy actions.
- **Out-of-combat SP** (Chinese 4.5): after combat, SP drops or recovers to two bars at 40 or 75 per second, and fully disengages after 3 s. Not in K1. Only matters for SP before and after fights.
- **Food restoring ultimate energy** (Chinese 4.6): 炖肉会议 and 小蜜虫布丁 restore a percentage of max ultimate energy. E8 covers food attributes only. Check food data.
- **Dive and finisher basic-attack bonus** (Chinese 1.3 note): normal-attack damage bonus does not affect the all-skill bonus, but does affect dives and finishers. Not confirmed either way (see Checks).

Incoming-only items (the source describes them, the engine does not simulate the enemy hitting operators): non-controlled damage reduction (1.13), operator defence and resistance from DEF and AGI/INT (1.10, 1.12), team Protection (1.7), enemy-applied statuses on operators (2.6: burn at 2% max HP ignoring DEF, stun, sprint lock, 3.5 s freeze), and 水灯虫's −10% operator DEF per stack (1.10).

## Agreements

- **D1, D3–D7 (zone structure):** Stagger ×1.3 and Link are multiplicative (1.11, 1.14); element, skill-type, Staggered and generic bonuses form one additive pool (1.3); Amp (1.5), Susceptibility (1.8) and damage taken (1.9) are each 1+Σ; Weakness and Protection do not affect outgoing damage (1.6, 1.7; see D26).
- **D2 (rounding):** the source's worked examples round to nearest (588.04 → 588, 119.23 → 119, 86.51 → 87, 105.79 → 106), which matches Math.round. Chinese 1.1 and 2.4 note that displayed attributes are floored (the engine floors only attributes in D10).
- **D8, D9, D10 (ATK formula):** ATK = [(base + weapon) × (1 + %) + flat] × (1 + 0.005 main + 0.002 sub), with the attributes floored (1.1, 4.2).
- **D11, E9 (gear lines):** fixed flat ATK on gear is not an integer (11 displays, 11.7167 actual; 16 displays, 16.1877 actual) and the flat slot comes before the attribute bonus (1.1). The source's example of a gloves ATK% shown as 12.3 but actually 12.25 is rounding, not floor. Check the data for the gloves value.
- **D12, D13 (base):** base damage plus a flat term (1.1, Industrial Explosives: ATK × 120% + 1250).
- **D15 (status zones):** the status formula (2.0) uses the element, amp, susceptibility, damage taken, crit, DEF, resistance, Stagger and Arts Intensity zones, and no skill-type zone or Link.
- **D19, A31 (Arts Intensity):** AI scales burst, reaction, Combustion, Shatter, Stagger-free status hits (2.4 list), not direct hits; status strength ×(1 + 2AI / (AI + 300)) for Electrification, Corrosion and Breach (2.4). The source's AI totals are not floored (41.4 + 29.4 = 70.8 used in its example): check the engine does not floor AI.
- **D20, D21, D22, D23 (level coefficients):** arts 1 + (L − 1)/196 = 1.4541 at 90; physical 1 + (L − 1)/392 = 1.2270 at 90 (2.3, 1.12 level data). Direct hits have no level coefficient (2.3). Crush takes the physical coefficient (2.2).
- **C1, C2, C3 (crit):** operator crit rate 5% and crit damage 50%; enemy crit 0%; crit zone 1 + crit damage on a crit, expected 1 + rate × damage (1.2).
- **R1, N2 (DEF):** enemy DEF 100 at every level, DEF factor 0.5 (1.10).
- **R4 (resistance):** resistance zone = 1 − res/100 + ignore/100 (1.12).
- **R5 (no floor, negative points):** the English 1.12 text says resistance points can act as negative in testing (Scorching Heart's ignore on a 0-point enemy gives ×1.2, "as if −20"), so the engine's no-floor rule matches.
- **R4/R8 (Laevatain's Scorching Heart):** ignore-resistance zone; 10/15/20 points for 20 s (English 1.12, Chinese 1.12).
- **N3 (enemy resistance tiers):** D 0, C 20 (30 for 潜地虬兽 natural), B 50 (1.12). Per-element points, level-independent. The table is labelled 1.0; not checked per enemy.
- **A2 (max stacks):** Arts infliction and Physical status both have a cap of 4 (2.1, 2.2). The source gives no duration for infliction.
- **A4, A5 (burst):** same element adds a stack and triggers a burst even at 4 stacks; burst 160% (2.1).
- **A7, A8 (reactions):** different element consumes all stacks; reaction level = stacks (1–4); initial hit 80% × (1 + level) (2.1, Cryo 80% stated).
- **A10 (forced reaction):** no initial damage (2.1).
- **A19, A20 (Combustion):** 10 s, 12% × (1 + level) Heat each second, ten ticks (2.1).
- **A25, A27 (Electrification):** Arts damage taken +12 / 16 / 20 / 24% for 12 / 18 / 24 / 30 s (2.1).
- **A26 (Electrification scope):** the source's "法术" = Heat, Electric, Cryo, Nature only (1.3, 2.1); Physical and Ether are not in it. This matches A26 (its OPEN tag can be narrowed, though the source gives no test for Ether).
- **A28, A29 (Corrosion):** 15 s; start 3.6 / 4.8 / 6 / 7.2 with +0.84 / 1.12 / 1.4 / 1.68 per second and caps 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 (2.1).
- **A32 (Corrosion refresh):** "refreshing keeps the earlier drop" (2.1). The ramp rule in A32 is not in the source.
- **A38 (Shatter):** 120% × (1 + level) Physical × Arts coefficient, on a Solidified enemy; applies to physical status and Vulnerable stack adds (2.1, 2.2). The source's "Vulnerable is unaffected" wording in A38 is unclear here: the source says 破防 application triggers it.
- **P1, P3 (Vulnerable):** max 4 stacks, plain Physical hit adds none; on an enemy without 破防, a physical status adds one stack and does no hit (2.2). Duration is not in the source.
- **P4, P5 (Lift, Knock Down):** hit 120% × (1 + AI) Physical, +1 stack (2.2). Status durations are not in the source.
- **P7 (Crush):** 150% × (1 + level) (2.2).
- **P8, P9 (Breach):** hit 50% × (1 + level) (2.2). Debuff 12 / 16 / 20 / 24% for 12 / 18 / 24 / 30 s (2.2, 2.4). The source confirms AI scaling on 碎甲 (2.4), so P9's AI-scaling OPEN tag can be narrowed. The engine's [ASSUMED] duration agrees with the source's values.
- **S7 (Staggered ×1.3):** 失衡易伤 coefficient 1.3, all enemies (1.11, 3.2).
- **S8 (no Stagger while Staggered):** not stated in the source.
- **S9 (Stagger duration):** normal 6 or 9 s, a few 11 s, 白垩核心 24 s (3.2). Data check: attribute 21 takes values 6–24 across templates, including 24 (the source's 白垩核心 value).
- **S11 (nodes):** most nodes at 50%; 白垩界卫 at 33% and 66% (3.4). The data has the same two values for 白垩界卫-type entries.
- **S13 (finisher only on Staggered):** source 3.3.
- **S14 (finisher multiplier):** Common 1, Advanced 1.25, Elite 1.5 (3.3); Boss and leader: see W9.
- **S15 (finisher SP):** 25 / 35 / 50 / 100 by rank (3.3, 4.5 table). The data's attribute for this is in `breakingAttackedAtbObtain`.
- **S2 (Stagger efficiency):** base × (1 + efficiency); only 点剑 gives +20% (3.1). Matches attribute 26 as a multiplier (default 1).
- **S3 (Stagger from kit hits):** heavy attacks, combo, battle skill and ultimate accumulate Stagger (3.1). The source's "Chen Qianyu's combo excluded" is not checked against the data (see Checks).
- **S1 (Stagger HP):** the data's attribute 20 by rank (data check, not per enemy): Common 60–110, Advanced 110–200, Elite 110–340, Boss 0–480 (rank 2 incl. non-staggering templates), Alpha 320–640. The source's ranges (Common 60–90, Advanced 140–200, Elite 280+) are broadly consistent; some Elite entries sit lower than the source's stated range.
- **K10, K11 (energy):** 6.5 team energy per 100 SP spent by a battle skill (4.6); combo grants 10 energy, multiplied by the energy-efficiency stat (4.6).
- **K5 (returned SP):** gives no energy and does not count as "restore" (4.5).
- **K1 (max 300, regen 8/s):** SP max 300 (3 bars) and regeneration 8/s in combat (4.5).
- **K32 (Link):** battle 30 / 45 / 60 / 75%, ultimate 20 / 30 / 40 / 50% at 1–4 stacks (1.14, English 1.14).
- **T18 (perfect dodge SP):** tested value about 7.5 (4.5). The engine's OPEN pair is 7 or 7.5; the source's test supports 7.5 (within "about").
- **B19 (zone add/multiply):** same-zone effects add, zones multiply (1.3–1.15 structure).
- **E8, E9, E6:** food and gear stats as panel lines (1.1, 4.2). Set and food details are not checked.
- **D26 (Weakness and Protection):** the source's formulas apply to the affected unit's outgoing damage (Weakness, enemy side) and incoming damage (Protection, team). Neither changes our outgoing damage, so ×1 is consistent. Weakness is only from 卡契尔's finisher (1.6).

## Source-internal conflicts (noted, not engine differences)

- **Xaihi's Cryo effect (1.9 vs 1.8):** Chinese 1.9 calls it 寒冷易伤 (damage taken, 易伤 zone, D6); English 2.6 list calls it "Cryo Fragility" (Susceptibility, 脆弱, D5). Check the data for the talent's attribute.
- **Corrosion scope:** see W5.
- **Boss finisher:** see W9.
- **Display rounding:** Chinese 1.1 says displayed values are floored, but its own 12.25 → 12.3 example is a rounding.

## Not covered by the source

- T (time): only the SP pause (K2/W8) touches time; no frame, hit-stop, haste, dilation or projectile claims.
- B (buffs): no timing, snapshot, stacking-key or layered-buff rules. Only the zone-additivity pattern.
- K (casting, except K1, K5, K10, K11, K32, T18): no cooldown, combo window, action gate, priority or chain rules.
- N (enemies, except N2, N3, N5): no enemy damage, enemy hit timing, shields, HP-conditioned effects (only 碾骨行刑人's DEF change, above) or control immunity.
- E (equipment, except E6, E8, E9): no set or weapon-skill rules.
- R9 (operator level): only the level coefficients (D20, D21).
- D9 per-point factors (attributes 76–79): talents may add attribute bonus; no factor given.
- D14 (fixed damage), D16–D18 (skill-type masks), D24, D27 (burst and reaction DMG attributes 35–38 and 56–59): not covered.
- Enemy crit, enemy stat growth (4.4): not used by the engine.

- Laevatain and Yvonne ultimate-state finisher exclusion (3.3): check kit data.
